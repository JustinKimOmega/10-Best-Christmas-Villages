                <h2>#1 — Santa Claus Village</h2>
                <p class="tagline">📍 Rovaniemi, Finland | The Home of Santa Claus</p>
                <img alt="Christmas scene in Santa Claus Village" class="place-image" src="../pictures/1.jpg">
                <h3>DESCRIPTION</h3>
                <p>Step into a winter wonderland at Santa Claus Village in Finnish Lapland. Visitors
                    can meet Santa, send postcards from the Santa Claus Main Post Office, and cross the
                    Arctic Circle. Snowy cabins and glowing lights make this a memorable Christmas
                    destination.</p>
                <div class="info">
                    <p><b> Things to Do: </b> Meet Santa Claus, cross the Arctic Circle, and explore festive shops.</p>
                    <p><b> Best Time to Visit: </b> November to December for the strongest Christmas
                        atmosphere; the village welcomes visitors year-round.</p>
                </div>
                <div class="travel-guide">
                    <h3>TRAVEL INFORMATION</h3>
                    <p><b> How to Get There: </b> Fly to Rovaniemi Airport, then use a local bus, taxi, or
                        arranged transfer to Santa Claus Village.</p>
                    <p><b> Travel Highlights: </b> Allow time to meet Santa, visit the post office, and enjoy the Arctic Circle area.</p>
                    <p><b> Travel Tips: </b> Winter clothing, insulated boots, gloves, and a warm hat are essential in snowy weather.</p>
                    <p><b> Important Note: </b> Market schedules, transportation services, and opening
                        hours may change each year. Check current details before your trip.</p>
