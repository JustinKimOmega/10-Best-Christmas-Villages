                <p class="tagline">📍 Alsace, France | A Storybook Christmas Town</p>

                <h3>DESCRIPTION</h3>
                <p>Colmar feels like a fairy tale during the holidays. Colorful half-timbered houses
                    sparkle with lights, and Christmas markets bring handmade crafts, festive food, and
                    warm drinks to the old town. Its Little Venice neighborhood is especially beautiful
                    in the evening.</p>
                <div class="info">
                    <p><b> Things to Do: </b> Walk through Old Town, explore Christmas markets, and see Little Venice.</p>
                    <p><b> Best Time to Visit: </b> Late November to December; confirm dates before traveling.</p>
                </div>
                <div class="travel-guide">
                    <h3>TRAVEL INFORMATION</h3>
                    <p><b> How to Get There: </b> Travel by train to Colmar, then explore the historic center on foot.</p>
                    <p><b> Travel Highlights: </b> Walk through the decorated old town, see the Christmas
                        markets, and admire the colorful half-timbered houses.</p>
                    <p><b> Travel Tips: </b> Wear comfortable walking shoes and layers for cold December weather.</p>
                    <p><b> Important Note: </b> Market schedules, transportation services, and opening
                        hours may change each year. Check current details before your trip.</p>
