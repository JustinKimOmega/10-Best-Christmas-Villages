                <h2>#3 — Rothenburg ob der Tauber</h2>
                <p class="tagline">📍 Bavaria, Germany | A Medieval Christmas Dream</p>
                <img alt="Christmas scene in Rothenburg ob der Tauber" class="place-image" src="../pictures/3.jpg">
                <h3>DESCRIPTION</h3>
                <p>This medieval town looks magical with cobblestone streets, historic houses, and a
                    cozy Christmas market. Visitors can browse traditional ornaments and enjoy the
                    peaceful atmosphere of the old town. Its charming architecture makes every corner
                    feel like a Christmas postcard.</p>
                <div class="info">
                    <p><b> Things to Do: </b> Visit the Reiterlesmarkt, discover the old town, and shop for ornaments.</p>
                    <p><b> Best Time to Visit: </b> Late November to December, during the Christmas market season.</p>
                </div>
                <div class="travel-guide">
                    <h3>TRAVEL INFORMATION</h3>
                    <p><b> How to Get There: </b> Take a train toward Rothenburg ob der Tauber, usually
                        with connections, then walk into the old town.</p>
                    <p><b> Travel Highlights: </b> Explore the medieval streets, Christmas market, and festive shop displays.</p>
                    <p><b> Travel Tips: </b> The cobblestone streets are easier to enjoy with sturdy shoes.</p>
                    <p><b> Important Note: </b> Market schedules, transportation services, and opening
                        hours may change each year. Check current details before your trip.</p>
