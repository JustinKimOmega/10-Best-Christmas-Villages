                <h3>DESCRIPTION</h3>
                <p>Old Quebec combines stone streets, historic buildings, and a beautiful snowy
                    atmosphere. During the Christmas season, the German Christmas Market features
                    wooden stalls, holiday gifts, and seasonal treats. The old city makes a wonderful
                    place for a winter walk.</p>
                <div class="info">
                    <p><b> Things to Do: </b> Explore the German Christmas Market and photograph the Old Town.</p>
                    <p><b> Best Time to Visit: </b> Late November to December for Christmas markets.</p>
                </div>
                <div class="travel-guide">
                    <h3>TRAVEL INFORMATION</h3>
                    <p><b> How to Get There: </b> Fly into Québec City or reach it by train or bus, then explore Old Québec on foot.</p>
                    <p><b> Travel Highlights: </b> Visit the decorated historic streets, nearby holiday
                        markets, and scenic viewpoints.</p>
                    <p><b> Travel Tips: </b> Bring a heavy coat, non-slip winter footwear, and extra layers.</p>
                    <p><b> Important Note: </b> Market schedules, transportation services, and opening
                        hours may change each year. Check current details before your trip.</p>
