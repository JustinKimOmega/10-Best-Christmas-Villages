                <p class="tagline">📍 Austria | An Alpine Christmas Postcard</p>
                <img alt="Christmas scene in Hallstatt" class="place-image" src="../pictures/8.jpg">
                <h3>DESCRIPTION</h3>
                <p>Hallstatt is a small lakeside village surrounded by dramatic mountains. In winter,
                    snowy rooftops and twinkling lights make the village look especially picturesque.
                    Visitors come for the scenery, peaceful streets, and traditional seasonal
                    atmosphere.</p>
                <div class="info">
                    <p><b> Things to Do: </b> Enjoy the lakeside view, stroll around the village, and take winter photos.</p>
                    <p><b> Best Time to Visit: </b> December for the Christmas mood; special market events
                        may take place only on selected dates.</p>
                </div>
                <div class="travel-guide">
                    <h3>TRAVEL INFORMATION</h3>
                    <p><b> How to Get There: </b> Travel by train to Hallstatt station and continue across
                        the lake by ferry when operating, or arrive by road.</p>
                    <p><b> Travel Highlights: </b> Admire lakeside scenery, explore the village streets,
                        and enjoy seasonal decorations.</p>
                    <p><b> Travel Tips: </b> Check winter ferry schedules, and allow extra travel time in mountain weather.</p>
                    <p><b> Important Note: </b> Market schedules, transportation services, and opening
                        hours may change each year. Check current details before your trip.</p>
