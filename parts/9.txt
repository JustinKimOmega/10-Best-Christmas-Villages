                <h2>#9 — Bruges</h2>
                <p class="tagline">📍 Belgium | Christmas by the Canals</p>
                <img alt="Christmas scene in Bruges" class="place-image" src="../pictures/9.jpg">
                <h3>DESCRIPTION</h3>
                <p>Bruges is known for its romantic canals, medieval buildings, and cobblestone
                    squares. Its winter festivities add lights, market stalls, and a cheerful holiday
                    atmosphere to the city. Walking around the historic center is one of the best ways
                    to appreciate its beauty.</p>
                <div class="info">
                    <p><b> Things to Do: </b> Explore the Markt, visit festive stalls, and see the canals after sunset.</p>
                    <p><b> Best Time to Visit: </b> Late November to early January for winter celebrations; check annual dates.</p>
                </div>
                <div class="travel-guide">
                    <h3>TRAVEL INFORMATION</h3>
                    <p><b> How to Get There: </b> Take a train to Bruges station and walk or use a local bus to the historic center.</p>
                    <p><b> Travel Highlights: </b> See the decorated squares, seasonal markets, canals, and medieval buildings.</p>
                    <p><b> Travel Tips: </b> Pack a rain-resistant jacket and comfortable shoes for walking.</p>
                    <p><b> Important Note: </b> Market schedules, transportation services, and opening
                        hours may change each year. Check current details before your trip.</p>
